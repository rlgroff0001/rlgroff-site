The Mentorship Roadmap

HOW THE FORGE OPERATES

A structured process designed for busy men who require depth, clarity, and accountability.

01 + Discernment Process

Matching & Vetting

Applications pair an Iron (Mentee) with a tested Anvil (Mentor) based on life stage and spiritual goals—preparing both to yield to the work of the Smith.

Apply as Iron or Anvil →
THE PAIRING COVENANT
  • 1. The Iron (Mentee): A man entering the heat, dropping passivity, and yielding to spiritual growth.
  • 2. The Anvil (Mentor): A tested leader offering a firm, unyielding foundation of truth and scripture.
  • 3. Discernment: Matched intentionally by vocation, spiritual season, and weekly availability.
  • 4. The Covenant: Strict mutual confidentiality, punctuality, and non-negotiable readiness.
02 + View 9-Week Breakdown

9-Week Guided Crucible

Execute a weekly curriculum focused on military-grade discipline, biblical stewardship, emotional resilience, and armor for spiritual warfare.

BetterMan Aligned Discipleship
THE 9-WEEK ROADMAP
Phase 1 Weeks 1–3
Real Manhood
  • W1: Rejecting Passivity: Stepping out of spiritual complacency.
  • W2: Accepting Responsibility: Owning leadership in heart, home, & church.
  • W3: Leading Courageously: Eternal reward over cultural approval.
Phase 2 Weeks 4–6
The Battle & The Heart
  • W4: Identity & Wounds: Addressing past hurts to lead with humility.
  • W5: Purity & Integrity: Establishing ironclad personal accountability.
  • W6: Relational Leadership: Loving selflessly in marriage & family.
Phase 3 Weeks 7–9
Mission & Deployment
  • W7: Vocation as Worship: Aligning work with kingdom stewardship.
  • W8: Spiritual Warfare: Recognizing tactics of enemy opposition.
  • W9: Commissioning: Completing the cohort and mentoring another.
03 + Battle Rhythm

Rhythm of Accountability

A structured 90-minute weekly meeting where the Anvil and Iron meet under Scripture for transparent confession, prayer, and long-term deployment.

90-MINUTE WEEKLY CADENCE
  • 15 Min: The Heat & Prayer: Grounding the session and bringing struggles into the light.
  • 45 Min: The Striking: Deep-diving into the 9-week scripture framework and application.
  • 30 Min: The Tempering: Transparent questioning on purity, family, and discipline.
  • Deployment: Once shaped, the Iron eventually steps into place as an Anvil.
