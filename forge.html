THE THREE PILLARS OF THE FORGE

Men aren't formed in isolation. They are tempered under intentional pressure, biblical alignment, and strategic accountability.

Biblical Stewardship & Vocation

Reframing your career, family, finances, and influence not as personal possessions, but as sacred trusts granted by God to be strategically managed for His Kingdom.

  • Kingdom Vocation Mapping
  • Spiritual Warfare Preparedness

Tactical One-to-One Accountability

Direct, high-trust mentor and mentee pairing. No passive consumption—only brutal honesty, shared prayer logs, purity accountability, and actionable weekly goals.

  • Daily Habit & Prayer Tracking
  • Direct Unfiltered Feedback

Battle-Tested Leadership

Drawing upon military command principles, social science research, and deep theological reflection to build men who remain steady in times of cultural shift and trial.

  • Command Discipline & Ethics
  • Generational Impact Focus

The Mentorship Roadmap

HOW THE FORGE OPERATES

A structured process designed for busy men who require depth, clarity, and accountability.

01

Matching & Vetting

Mentors and Mentees are intentionally evaluated based on life stage, leadership goals, theological alignment, and spiritual growth targets.

02

9-Week Guided Crucible

Execute a weekly curriculum focused on military-grade discipline, biblical stewardship, emotional resilience, and armor for spiritual warfare.

03

Rhythm of Accountability

Pairs complete brief daily digital logs (prayer, scripture, habits) and convene for an intense 45-minute weekly debrief session.
