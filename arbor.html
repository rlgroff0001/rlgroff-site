Grounded Foundation

THE THREE ARBOR PILLARS

1. Vocation & Steward Mindset

Transform your 20s from passive career-seeking into strategic Kingdom stewardship—budgeting, work ethic, and directional vision.

2. Purity & High Trust

Direct 1-to-1 mentorship pairing with an older mentor. Unfiltered honesty regarding purity, habits, and digital discipline.

3. Relational Readiness

Preparing to lead a future family before dating. Developing emotional maturity, biblical courtship standards, and selfless leadership.

9-Week Curriculum Arc

THE SINGLE MAN'S ROADMAP

Phase 1 • Weeks 1–3

Establishing the Roots

  • W1: Rejecting Drift: Establishing daily spiritual discipline & morning rhythms.
  • W2: Financial Freedom: Budgeting, debt elimination, & generous giving in your 20s.
  • W3: Vocation as Worship: Building deep work habits and career intentionality.
Phase 2 • Weeks 4–6

Internal Integrity

  • W4: Purity & Screen Safeguards: Eradicating secret habits & building total transparency.
  • W5: Emotional Maturity: Overcoming father-wounds, anger, & passive isolation.
  • W6: Brotherhood: Moving from casual acquaintances to high-accountability allies.
Phase 3 • Weeks 7–9

Mission & Marriage Preparedness

  • W7: Preparing for Marriage: What it takes to be a husband before you date.
  • W8: Intentional Courtship: Biblical standards vs. modern hookup culture.
  • W9: Commissioning: Stepping into leadership in church & community.
