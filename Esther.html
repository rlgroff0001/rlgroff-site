Core Foundations

THE THREE ESTHER PILLARS

1. Identity & Grace

Rooting your self-worth in Christ—breaking free from perfectionism, people-pleasing, cultural comparison, and performance anxiety.

2. Titus 2 Mentorship

Direct 1-to-1 pairing between seasoned women leaders and mentees. High confidentiality, deep prayer, and honest life processing.

3. Strategic Calling

Stewarding your unique influence, career, family, and spiritual gifts with courage to impact your community for the Kingdom.

Curriculum Blueprint

THE 9-WEEK DISCIPLESHIP ARC

Phase 1 • Weeks 1–3

Anchored Identity

  • W1: Daughter of the King: Dismantling performance-based worth.
  • W2: The Secret Place: Cultivating personal prayer & scripture delight.
  • W3: Emotional Health: Processing past hurt, comparison, & anxiety.
Phase 2 • Weeks 4–6

Relational Wisdom

  • W4: Healthy Boundaries: Honor, communication, & life balance.
  • W5: Purity & Integrity: Guarding the heart in relationships & media.
  • W6: Authentic Sisterhood: Deep community over isolation.
Phase 3 • Weeks 7–9

Courageous Influence

  • W7: Vocation as Worship: Bringing Kingdom light into your workplace.
  • W8: Spiritual Warfare: Standing in intercessory prayer & discernment.
  • W9: Commissioning: Stepping forward to lead & mentor others.
